Arrange by

It replaces the rail's Colour by card. Planet size is always the memory count; only orbit distance changes. Each planet keeps its angle, so you can still find it.

Activity
writes + recalls in the last 30 days (default)
Size
memory count; biggest closest
Age
median age of live memories; youngest closest
Recalls
lifetime recalls; most recalled closest

Activity needs a daily tally per project (MAPX.3): the 1 MB activity log holds only about two days. Until the tally has 30 days, lifetime numbers stand in.

Drill-down

  1. Galaxy: sun and nodules (both turn), planets on orbits, belts, black holes with energy flows.
  2. Planet: the camera flies in; land masses labelled, kind moons with swarms, black holes named by partner.
  3. Moon: the swarm spreads into a readable ring.
  4. Memory: knowledge satellites and the detail panel, with Edit, Pin and Retire from the dashboard writes.

Each step is a camera flight: it speeds into the body you clicked, with star streaks, and the next level settles in; the path bar flies back out. Wheel, +/− and drag zoom and pan at any level. The URL hash keeps the level across reloads.

Rules and caps

  1. Orbits are ordered rings by rank; ring gaps grow with planet size, so planets never overlap.
  2. A project under 3 memories is a dwarf planet: small, no moons, its memories in its belt; it keeps its rank's ring.
  3. At most 12 land masses per planet; the rest are small islands. At most 8 moons.
  4. A black hole needs 2 or more similar pairs (cosine 0.80 or more) between two projects.
  5. Asteroids appear only at Planet level and deeper; at Galaxy level a swarm is a haze.
  6. Every body has a tooltip: planets (style, counts, top tags, black holes), land and bands (tag, uses, shared planets), moons, asteroids, belts, black holes, satellites.
  7. Motion pauses on hover or selection; reduced motion stops orbits and flights; Tab moves sun, planets, moons, memories.
  8. Colours from a colour-blind-safe palette; a tag's hue family comes from its namespace.
